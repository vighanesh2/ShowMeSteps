Privacy

How we handle recordings, learner attempts, and team data.

What we store

Tutorial content, redacted interaction recordings, screenshots (with passwords masked), learner attempt results, and team membership for business accounts.

What we don’t store

Passwords and sensitive form values are redacted during recording. Error monitoring is configured not to send personal data (PII) by default.

Your controls

  • Team admins can hide member emails from non-admins
  • Retention windows for attempt data (purge expired)
  • Toggle AI coaching features per team
  • API: /api/privacy/preferences and delete/purge endpoints
